Simplified conference logo options for conference swag.

Five directions for a swag-ready mark, each built from scratch as clean vector art. The full FTNN badge — a text ring wrapped around six interlocking figures — is beautiful on screen but loses its detail on an embroidered cap, an engraved tumbler, or a 1-inch pin. These concepts keep the heart of the mark while surviving the reduction. Toggle One color up top to judge how each reads with a single thread or engraving color.

October 13–14, 2026
Hyatt Regency McCormick Place · Chicago
Theme: One Heart, Many Hands

Why simplify the mark for swag

The current badge carries a lot of fine detail — an outer text ring, the “EST 2015” markers, and six two-tone figures with interlocking arms. At full size it’s gorgeous. Shrunk to a cap front (~2.5″), a pin (1″), or laser-etched on steel, the thin lines fill in, the two blues muddy together, and the wordmark ring becomes unreadable.

Every concept below is drawn to hold up at small sizes and in a single color, while still reading as FTNN and the 2026 Chicago conference.

For the Board — how to use this

Each concept is real vector art, not a sketch. Once the Board narrows to one or two directions, I’ll deliver production files (SVG, EPS, and PNG in color / one-color / reversed) sized for the vendor, plus the conference lockups. A quick recommendation to frame the discussion:

Safest bet
Concept 2 · The Trinity

The most reliable across every item and size — the one that will never look muddy on a hat or a pin.

Most flexible
Concept 4 · Monogram

Pairs with any of the icon marks and works where an emblem won’t fit — sleeves, left chest, cuffs.

Most memorable
Concept 3 · Heart of Hands

The strongest tie to this year’s theme — ideal if the Board wants swag that feels specific to Chicago 2026.
